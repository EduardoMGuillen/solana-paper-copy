How it works

Three steps. No browser extension, no web wallet connection, no custody.

01

Install and import

Download the app for Windows or Linux and import a dedicated wallet. The key is written only to a private file on your computer.

02

Pick a profile

Choose Recommended, Conservative or Aggressive, or tune your own filters, exits and position size. Hard limits live in your config file.

03

Press START

Every 15 seconds the bot screens Meteora pools, opens Bid-Ask positions in SOL and exits by rule. STOP closes everything.

Features

Built for people who want DLMM fees without staring at charts all day.

STRATEGY

Single-sided SOL, Bid-Ask

Liquidity sits below the price and earns fees as the market trades through it. You never buy the token up front.

SCREENING

Fee-first pool picking

Filters by TVL, 24 h fees, base fee, hourly volume and pool age, then ranks by fees per dollar of liquidity.

EXITS

Automatic exits

Take profit, stop loss, max hold time and sudden-drop protection, measured on the position's real on-chain value.

SECURITY

Keys never leave your PC

No servers, no custody. The key never travels over the network; the app signs locally with a wallet used only for the bot.

CONTROL

Telegram and kill switch

Status digests and commands on Telegram, per-position close buttons and an emergency stop file.

DESKTOP

Runs in the tray

Close the window and the bot keeps watching your positions. Bring your own RPC (Helius, QuickNode) for speed.

FAQ

Do you ever hold my funds or keys?

No. The app runs on your computer and signs locally. There is no PolloTraderBot server and nothing to log into.

Can I lose money?

Yes. If a token drops, a Bid-Ask position turns into that token and loses value. Stop loss limits the damage but cannot remove it. Only use funds you can afford to lose.

Does it keep running when my PC is off?

Positions stay open on Meteora and keep earning fees, but exits only run while the app is open. Keep it running, press STOP before shutting down, or run it on a server.

How much SOL do I need?

Each position needs about 0.1 SOL of refundable rent plus its stake. Around 1 SOL is a comfortable start.
